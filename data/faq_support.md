# Enterprise Support FAQ
Author: Shivanshu
 
## Refund Policy
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds take 5-7 business days to process to the original payment method.
 
## Account Lockout
If an account is locked due to multiple failed login attempts, the user must wait 15 minutes or contact support for an immediate password reset link.
 
## Subscription Upgrade and Downgrade
Mid-billing cycle upgrades are prorated. The user will be charged the difference between the new and old tier for the remaining days in the month. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
 
## Server Downtime SLA
Enterprise customers are guaranteed 99.9% uptime. If downtime exceeds this SLA in a calendar month, customers are eligible for a 10% credit on their next invoice.
